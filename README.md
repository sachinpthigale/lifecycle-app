## 🔍 How Semantic Multi-Level Zoom Works

```
[ Macro View ]   ───●───────────────●──────────────────●────────────────●───
(High-level)       18              30                 45               65
                   Adulthood       Family Launch      Midlife Health   Retirement

                          ▼ ZOOM IN (Decade View) ▼

[ Decade View ]  ───●────●────●─────●────●─────────────●────●───────────●────●──
                   18   22   25    30   32            40   45          62   65
                   Adult Job  Fund  Fam  529           Card Colon       SS   Med

                          ▼ ZOOM IN (Detailed View) ▼

[ Detailed View] ──●──●──●──●─●─●───●──●──────────────●────●───────────●─●──●──
                   18 19 22 25 31 32 33 35            40   45          62 64 65
                               (Daycare, 529, Will & Guardianship, etc.)
```

### 1. Zoom Level 1: Macro View (Zoomed Out)
- **Focus**: High-level life epochs.
- **Events**: Only 4 core pillars across life (*18 Adulthood*, *30 Family Foundations*, *45 Mid-Career Health*, *65 Retirement*).
- **Spacing**: Maximum horizontal breathing room. Zero clutter.

### 2. Zoom Level 2: Decade View (Default)
- **Focus**: Key 5-to-10-year milestones.
- **Events**: 11 milestones (*22 First Job & 800 Credit*, *25 Emergency Buffer*, *32 529 College Plan & Life Insurance*, *40 Cardiac Baseline*, *50 Catch-Up*, *62 Social Security*, *70 Living Trust*).
- **Anti-Collision**: Staggered alternating stems (odd labels above, even labels below).

### 3. Zoom Level 3: Detailed View (Zoomed In)
- **Focus**: Granular micro-milestones that normally collide.
- **Events**: Expands the track to **4,800px+**, revealing 21 milestones:
  - *Age 31*: Daycare Waitlists & Infant Care
  - *Age 32*: 529 College Plan & Term Life Insurance
  - *Age 33*: Last Will & Child Legal Guardianship
  - *Age 35*: Preschool & Elementary School District Boundaries
  - *Age 38*: Extracurriculars & 529 Asset Allocation Mid-Check
  - *Age 48*: High School & College Campus Tours
  - *Age 55*: Aging Parents & Healthcare Proxies
  - *Age 64½*: Medicare Initial 7-Month Countdown Window
- **No Overlap**: At 92px width per year, adjacent years (31, 32, 33) have ample room, reinforced by alternating top/bottom label stems.

## 👆 Mobile Gestures & Controls

1. **Touch Pinch-to-Zoom**: Pinch open with 2 fingers to zoom in; pinch closed to zoom out.
2. **Quick Level Pills**: Tap **Macro**, **Decade**, or **Detailed** in the top bar.
3. **Floating + / − Buttons**: 1-tap zooming on the bottom action pill.
4. **Center Retention**: When zooming in or out, your active life stage stays centered on screen.
5. **Inspect Dot**: Tap any dot to slide up the clean sheet (*What Happens*, *What Peers Do*, *Essential To-Dos*).
